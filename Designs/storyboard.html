Distance-ranked, not alphabetical. The whole point of "nearby" is that the rider shouldn't have to think — the closest relevant stop leads.
Color carries status, not decoration. Green means catch it, yellow means hurry, and the delay is stated in real minutes — never just a vague icon.
The track is the whole story. Passed stops fade, the live position shows on the line itself, so the rider never has to interpret a map pin.
Favourites remove the search step entirely for the handful of stops someone actually uses daily.
Search lives on its own tab. Tapping it opens a focused, full-screen view with recent searches ready — no search box cluttering the other screens.
